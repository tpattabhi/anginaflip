# E1: Cleveland vs. Detrano et al. 1986

Source: Detrano R, et al. *Circulation* 1986;73(5):970–977, doi:10.1161/01.CIR.73.5.970 (free access at ahajournals.org). The published Tables 1, 2 and 8 are compared with the UCI Cleveland file.

The PDF and the table images are kept locally and are not redistributed in the repository.

`prior_reports.md` records the novelty searches.

The check is in `code/evidence_checks.py` (`check_detrano1986`) and is tested in `tests/test_evidence.py`.
